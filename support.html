Common questions

Why do Fasli, Shenshai and Kadmi show different dates?

These are distinct Zoroastrian calendar traditions. Fasli in this release uses a fixed March 21 New Year with its separate leap-day rule; the 365-day Shenshai and Kadmi calendars drift relative to the seasons. Choose your tradition in Settings. Iranian cultural or national events may use a Shamsi date independently; an event’s details identify its tradition and source.

Which date range can I use?

Date selection, conversion and prayer-time estimates support 1800–2100. The event collection is curated; it does not claim to include every regional custom or establish that a modern observance existed in every historical year.

Why can prayer times differ from my community schedule?

Prayer boundaries depend on the selected location and convention. RojMah offers a civil-clock convention, a Hoshbam option and a separately marked Bombay-time option for Mumbai. Estimated sunrise and sunset use standard horizon and refraction assumptions; local terrain, weather and community practice can differ. At polar day or night the app shows that a normal solar schedule is unavailable. Main calendar dates begin at midnight.

How do I enable or troubleshoot reminders?

Enable notifications in RojMah Settings and grant the system permission. Choose the prayer periods or event categories you want, the event reminder time and the lead time. Check the reminder status for permission errors and the scheduled horizon. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the system holds a limited number of upcoming reminders. Open RojMah regularly or tap its renewal reminder to replenish them. Focus modes and system notification settings can affect presentation.

Can I use the app without an internet connection?

Yes. The first App Store release includes its event collection, fonts and calendar calculations, so its main features work offline. Opening source references, support or privacy pages uses your browser and requires internet access. Remote dataset updates are not configured in the default first release.

How do I switch language, appearance or calendar?

Open Settings to choose English or Persian, light or dark appearance, the Zoroastrian calendar system, the prayer location and prayer convention. Main navigation remains available when these preferences change.

How do I manage favorites or back up settings?

Use the star on an event to favorite it, then use the Favorites filter to find it again. Settings offers a JSON settings backup that you can copy and later import. This backup contains app preferences; it does not include favorites. Resetting settings keeps favorites and cached event data.

How can I remove local data?

Reset Settings to restore preferences, unstar events to clear favorites and use Restore bundled data to replace a downloaded event collection. Notifications can be disabled in the app or system settings. Removing the app and its stored data removes its local state; system backups and copies you exported are controlled separately by you and your operating system.

What is the OBS overlay on Mac?

The optional desktop overlay serves date and event information at a loopback address on the same computer for use as an OBS Browser Source. It does not upload an overlay to a public server. If you broadcast or record it, your chosen streaming or recording service determines where the visible information goes. Turn it off in Settings when you no longer need it.

پشتیبانی روزماه

برای دریافت کمک یا گزارش خطا در تاریخ‌ها، به mim3dot@gmail.com ایمیل بزنید. نسخهٔ برنامه، مدل دستگاه، نسخهٔ سیستم‌عامل و مراحل بروز مشکل را ذکر کنید. برای اصلاح تاریخ، تاریخ میلادی، نوع تقویم انتخاب‌شده و منبع معتبر را نیز بفرستید.

تقویم‌های فصلی، شاهنشاهی و قدیمی سنت‌های متفاوتی دارند. نوع تقویم و شیوهٔ محاسبهٔ اوقات نیایش را در تنظیمات انتخاب کنید؛ توضیح هر رویداد، سنت و منبع آن را نشان می‌دهد. اوقات طلوع و غروب تخمینی‌اند و ممکن است با شرایط محلی یا برنامهٔ جامعهٔ شما تفاوت داشته باشند.

قابلیت‌های اصلی نسخهٔ نخست بدون اینترنت کار می‌کنند. یادآورها نیاز به اجازهٔ اعلان دارند؛ برای تازه‌سازی یادآورهای آینده، برنامه را مرتب باز کنید. بازهٔ پشتیبانی‌شدهٔ تاریخ‌ها ۱۸۰۰ تا ۲۱۰۰ میلادی است.
